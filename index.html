SMALL CHANGES, CLEANER AIR

Indoor air, improved

4 practical habits
⌂

Time your ventilation

Ventilate when outdoor air is cleaner. Keep windows closed during nearby smoke, heavy traffic or a pollution spike.

✧

Filter the air

Consider a correctly sized HEPA air cleaner in the room where your family spends the most time.

⌁

Keep particles down

Wet-mop and damp-dust. Avoid incense, candles, smoking and high-emission cooking where possible.

♧

Make a clean-air room

Choose one room, reduce indoor sources and run filtration consistently when outdoor air is poor.
